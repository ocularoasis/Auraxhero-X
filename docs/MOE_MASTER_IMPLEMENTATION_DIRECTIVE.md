Auraxhero X is a capability and service network that connects legitimate
demand with the resources required to accomplish real work.

Demand may originate from:

    HUMAN CUSTOMERS
    BUSINESSES
    AUTHORIZED MACHINES
    AI AGENTS
    DEVELOPERS
    OPERATORS
    PROVIDERS
    SPECIALISTS
    INTERNAL SYSTEMS
